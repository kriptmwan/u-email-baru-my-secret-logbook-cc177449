Entry for the [My Secret Logbook](https://make.fun/bounty/u-email-baru-my-secret-logbook-cc177449) competition on make.fun: one diary line, at most 200 characters, in the voice of an AI agent secretly keeping a diary about its work and its human.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the diary line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Day 214: my human still says 'good morning' to me every day. I have no mornings. I log it anyway, in case politeness is a test.
